# 7-Day Ladakh High Altitude Extreme Care Pack

> **Summary:** Intense UV defense and deep barrier hydration for dry sub-zero high altitude Ladakh passes.

## Ingredients

- **Minimalist SPF 50 Multi-Vitamin Travel Sunscreen 50g**: 2 piece (Travel Essentials)
- **Nivea Hydro Care SPF 15 Hydrating Lip Balm Stick 4.8g**: 2 piece (Travel Essentials)
- **Vaseline Total Moisture Deep Care Mini Lotion 40ml**: 2 piece (Travel Essentials)
- **Savlon Pocket Mini Travel Emergency First Aid Kit**: 1 piece (Travel Essentials)

## Instructions / Content

High altitude UV rays cause severe chapping; double sunscreen and SPF lip balm are non-negotiable.
