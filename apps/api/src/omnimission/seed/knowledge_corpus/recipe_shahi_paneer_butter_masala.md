# Restaurant-Style Shahi Paneer Butter Masala

> **Summary:** Velvety cashew tomato gravy simmered with fresh malai paneer cubes, butter, and kasuri methi.

## Ingredients

- **Amul Fresh Malai Paneer 200g Vacuum Pack**: 400 g (Paneer & Tofu)
- **Amul Pasteurised Salted Butter Block**: 50 g (Butter & Margarine)
- **Amul Pure Low Fat Fresh Dairy Cream**: 100 ml (Cream & Whipping)
- **Nutraj Mangalore Black Pepper Roasted Cashews**: 50 g (Dry Fruits & Nuts)
- **MDH Aromatic Dried Fenugreek Leaves (Kasuri Methi)**: 10 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Everest Kashmiri Lal Stemless Mild Red Chilli Powder**: 15 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Catch Superb 13-Spice Garam Masala Powder**: 5 g (Spices & Salt)

## Instructions / Content

A rich Mughlai vegetarian delicacy.
1. Gravy Base: Boil tomatoes, cashews, and whole spices for 15 minutes, then blend into a silky smooth puree.
2. Tempering: Melt butter in pan, add Kashmiri chilli powder and the strained tomato-cashew puree.
3. Simmer: Cook on low flame for 12 minutes until butter releases on edges.
4. Finish: Add fresh malai paneer cubes, fresh dairy cream, crushed kasuri methi, and garam masala.
