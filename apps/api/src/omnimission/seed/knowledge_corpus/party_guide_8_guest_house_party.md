# House Party Kit for 8 Guests (Snacks, Drinks & Tableware)

> **Summary:** Zero-stress hosting checklist calculating exact drink cans, snack portions, compostable cups, and cleaning bags for 8 people.

## Ingredients

- **Origami Biodegradable Paper Beverage Cups (Pack of 50)**: 1 piece (Disposables)
- **Origami Heavy Duty Compostable Dinner Plates 10-inch (Pack of 25)**: 1 piece (Disposables)
- **Paseo Luxury 2-Ply Party Table Napkins (Pack of 100)**: 1 piece (Disposables)
- **Origami Compostable Birchwood Party Spoons & Forks (Pack of 50)**: 1 piece (Disposables)
- **Schweppes Spiced Ginger Ale Can 300ml (Pack of 6)**: 2 piece (Soft Drinks & Mixers)
- **Coca-Cola Original Refreshing Taste Can 300ml (Pack of 6)**: 2 piece (Soft Drinks & Mixers)
- **Lay's Classic Salted Potato Chips Big Sharing Bag 115g**: 3 piece (Chips)
- **Doritos Cheese Supreme Crunchy Tortilla Nachos 150g**: 2 piece (Chips)
- **Cantina Mexican Spicy Tomato Chunky Salsa Dip 250g**: 1 piece (Dips & Sauces)
- **Haldiram's Nagpur Aloo Bhujia Spicy Crunchy Snack 400g**: 1 piece (Namkeen)
- **Shalimar Premium Oxo-Biodegradable Dustbin Trash Bags Medium (Pack of 30)**: 1 piece (Cleaning Essentials)

## Instructions / Content

Hosting formula for 8 people:
- Beverage formula: 3-4 drinks per guest = ~24 cans (2x Ginger Ale 6-packs + 2x Cola 6-packs).
- Snack formula: 100g chips/nachos per person = ~800g total (3x Lays + 2x Doritos + 1x Salsa dip).
- Disposable buffer: 3 cups per guest = 24-30 cups (1 pack of 50 covers the full evening).
- Post-party waste: Biodegradable trash bags make cleanup take under 5 minutes.
