# Fresh Eggless Strawberry Cream Cake

> **Summary:** Soft moist vanilla sponge layered with Mahabaleshwar strawberry crush, whipped non-dairy cream, and fresh strawberry slices.

## Ingredients

- **Maida Premium Refined Flour**: 250 g (Flours)
- **Superfine Powdered Sugar**: 180 g (Sugars & Sweeteners)
- **Double Action Baking Powder**: 10 g (Baking Essentials)
- **Pure Edible Baking Soda**: 5 g (Baking Essentials)
- **Amul Masti Dahi Curd**: 200 g (Yogurt & Curd)
- **Fortune Sunlite Refined Sunflower Cooking Oil**: 80 ml (Edible Oils)
- **Sprig Natural Bourbon Vanilla Extract**: 5 ml (Essence & Flavors)
- **Fresh Mahabaleshwar Sweet Strawberries**: 200 g (Fresh Fruits)
- **Mapro Whole Fruit Strawberry Crush**: 100 ml (Fruit Crushes & Syrups)
- **Rich's Non-Dairy Whip Topping Cream**: 300 ml (Cream & Whipping)
- **Oddy Ecobake Non-Stick Baking Parchment Paper**: 1 piece (Kitchen Disposables)
- **HomeBasics Anodized Aluminium Round Cake Pan 8-inch**: 1 piece (Bakeware)

## Instructions / Content

An authentic bakery-style layered strawberry gateau prepared without eggs.
1. Sponge Aeration: Whisk curd and powdered sugar until smooth, then add baking powder and baking soda. Let rest for 5 minutes until frothy bubbles form.
2. Wet & Dry Fold: Gently fold in oil, vanilla extract, and sifted maida flour without over-beating.
3. Bake: Pour into parchment-lined 8-inch cake tin and bake at 180°C for 30-35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
4. Assembly: Slice cooled sponge into 2 layers. Soak each layer with water-diluted strawberry crush. Pipe chilled whipped cream, lay fresh strawberry slices, and frost the exterior.
