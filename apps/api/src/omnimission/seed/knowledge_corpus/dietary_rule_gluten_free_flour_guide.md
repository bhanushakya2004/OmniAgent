# Gluten-Free Flour Selection & Cross-Contamination Rules

> **Summary:** Rules for choosing gluten-free flours and binders (almond, oat, tapioca, rice) and avoiding wheat cross-contact.

## Ingredients


## Instructions / Content

Gluten provides elasticity and traps gas in baked goods. To replace wheat flour safely:
1. Almond Flour: 100% grain-free blanched ground almonds. High protein and healthy fats. Best for soft cakes, macarons, and keto baking.
2. Certified Gluten-Free Oat Flour: Rich in soluble fiber, gives familiar bakery chewiness. Must be certified gluten-free to avoid cross-contamination in grain mills.
3. Tapioca Starch / Corn Starch: Added in 15-20% ratio to gluten-free flour blends to mimic gluten's stretch and prevent crumbly texture.
4. Xanthan Gum or Psyllium Husk: Add 1/2 tsp per cup of gluten-free flour to provide tensile strength.
