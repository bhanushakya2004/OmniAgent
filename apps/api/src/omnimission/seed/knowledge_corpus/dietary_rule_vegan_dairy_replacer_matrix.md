# Vegan Dairy Replacer Matrix for Indian Cooking

> **Summary:** Complete ingredient swap table replacing dairy milk, paneer, butter, and yogurt in traditional Indian dishes.

## Ingredients


## Instructions / Content

1. Milk: Swap cow milk with Oatly Barista Oat milk for chai/coffee, or Raw Pressery Almond milk for desserts.
2. Paneer: Swap with firm organic Tofu for 1:1 protein and texture in palak paneer and tikkas.
3. Butter: Swap with cold-pressed coconut oil or neutral sunflower oil in baking and rotis.
4. Curd/Dahi: Swap with coconut yogurt or cashew curd for marinades and cake aeration.
