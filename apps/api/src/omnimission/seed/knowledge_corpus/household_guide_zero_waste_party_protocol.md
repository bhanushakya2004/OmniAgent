# Eco-Friendly Zero-Plastic Party Protocol

> **Summary:** How to host an eco-conscious gathering without plastic cups, single-use polythene, or microplastics.

## Ingredients

- **Origami Compostable Birchwood Party Spoons & Forks (Pack of 50)**: 1 piece (Disposables)
- **Origami Heavy Duty Compostable Dinner Plates 10-inch (Pack of 25)**: 1 piece (Disposables)
- **GreenStalk Heavy Duty 3-Ply Paper Drinking Straws (Pack of 50)**: 1 piece (Disposables)

## Instructions / Content

Use sugarcane bagasse plates and birchwood cutlery that degrade naturally in compost within 90 days.
