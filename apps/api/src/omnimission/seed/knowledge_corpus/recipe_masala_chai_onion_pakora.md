# Monsoon Masala Chai & Crispy Onion Pakoras

> **Summary:** Hot spiced ginger cardamom tea paired with crunchy sliced onion fritters fried in besan batter.

## Ingredients

- **Brooke Bond Red Label Natural Care Masala Chai**: 30 g (Tea & Coffee)
- **Amul Taaza Homogenised Toned Milk**: 300 ml (Milk)
- **Tata Sampann Unpolished Bengal Gram Chana Dal**: 150 g (Pulses & Dals)
- **Fortune Sunlite Refined Sunflower Cooking Oil**: 300 ml (Edible Oils)

## Instructions / Content

1. Brew CTC tea with crushed fresh ginger, cardamom, and milk for 5 minutes.
2. Slice onions thin; toss with besan (gram flour), ajwain, chilli powder, and fresh coriander with minimal water.
3. Drop into hot sunflower oil and deep fry until dark golden and crispy.
