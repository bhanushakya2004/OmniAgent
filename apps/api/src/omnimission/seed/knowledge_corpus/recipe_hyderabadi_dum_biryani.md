# Authentic Hyderabadi Royal Dum Biryani

> **Summary:** Fragrant layered basmati rice and marinated paneer cooked in sealed dum pot with saffron, mint, and desi ghee.

## Ingredients

- **India Gate Classic Aged Long Grain Basmati Rice**: 500 g (Rice)
- **Amul Fresh Malai Paneer**: 300 g (Paneer & Tofu)
- **Amul Pure Cow Milk Desi Ghee**: 80 ml (Ghee & Butter)
- **Everest Shahi Royal Biryani Masala**: 30 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Amul Masti Dahi Pasteurised Curd**: 150 g (Yogurt & Curd)
- **Dabur Natural Kewra Water Aroma Essence**: 10 ml (Essence & Flavors)

## Instructions / Content

1. Parboil aged basmati rice with whole cloves, cardamom, and cinnamon until 70% cooked.
2. Layer marinated spiced paneer at the base of heavy pot.
3. Layer rice, drizzle saffron milk, desi ghee, kewra water, and fried onions.
4. Seal pot lid with dough and cook on low heat for 25 minutes.
