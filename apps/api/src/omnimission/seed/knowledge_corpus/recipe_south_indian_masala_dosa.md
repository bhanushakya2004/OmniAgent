# Crispy South Indian Potato Masala Dosa

> **Summary:** Thin golden fermented rice-crepe stuffed with spiced turmeric mustard potato bhaji and served with coconut chutney.

## Ingredients

- **Buhler Cleaned Premium Sona Masoori Raw Rice**: 400 g (Rice)
- **Tata Sampann Skinned Whole White Urad Dal**: 100 g (Pulses & Dals)
- **Tata Sampann Whole Fenugreek Seeds**: 10 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Amul Pasteurised Salted Butter Block**: 100 g (Butter & Margarine)

## Instructions / Content

1. Soak rice, urad dal, and methi seeds for 6 hours. Grind to silky batter and ferment for 12 hours.
2. Cook potato stuffing with mustard seeds, curry leaves, ginger, and turmeric.
3. Pour batter on hot cast-iron tava, swirl thin, dot with butter, fill with potato bhaji, and roll.
