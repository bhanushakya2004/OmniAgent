# Vegan Gluten-Free Blueberry Breakfast Muffins

> **Summary:** Hearty wholesome morning muffins loaded with wild blueberries, rolled oat flour, and flaxseed egg replacement.

## Ingredients

- **True Elements Rolled Oat Flour Certified Gluten Free**: 200 g (Flours)
- **True Elements Cold Milled Golden Flaxseed Powder**: 20 g (Superfoods)
- **Oatly Barista Edition Gluten Free Oat Milk**: 180 ml (Plant Milk)
- **Fortune Sunlite Refined Sunflower Cooking Oil**: 60 ml (Edible Oils)
- **Fresh Imported Premium Blueberries Punnet**: 125 g (Fresh Fruits)
- **Double Action Baking Powder**: 8 g (Baking Essentials)
- **Trust Classic Natural Demerara Brown Sugar**: 80 g (Sugars & Sweeteners)

## Instructions / Content

Golden vegan morning muffins with bursting pockets of warm blueberries.
1. Make Flax Egg: Combine 2 tablespoons flaxseed powder with 6 tablespoons warm oat milk. Let sit 10 minutes to thicken.
2. Combine dry gluten-free oat flour, brown sugar, and baking powder.
3. Mix flax egg, sunflower oil, and remaining oat milk. Fold together gently.
4. Fold in fresh blueberries and spoon into muffin tin. Bake at 190°C for 22 minutes.
