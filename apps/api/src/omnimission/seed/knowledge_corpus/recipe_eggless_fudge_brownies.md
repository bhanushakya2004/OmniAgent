# Classic Eggless Chewy Dark Chocolate Brownies

> **Summary:** Crinkly top, ultra-fudgy dark chocolate brownies made without eggs using condensed milk and melted dark chocolate.

## Ingredients

- **Aashirvaad Select Maida Premium Refined Flour**: 150 g (Flours)
- **Morde Real 55% Dark Chocolate Drops for Baking**: 200 g (Chocolate & Candies)
- **Amul Pasteurised Salted Butter Block**: 100 g (Butter & Margarine)
- **Nestle Milkmaid Sweetened Condensed Milk**: 200 g (Baking Essentials)
- **Cadbury Rich Cocoa Powder**: 30 g (Baking Essentials)

## Instructions / Content

1. Melt dark chocolate and butter together over double boiler.
2. Whisk in condensed milk and vanilla extract.
3. Sift flour and cocoa powder; fold gently into chocolate mixture.
4. Pour into lined square pan and bake at 170°C for 22 minutes.
