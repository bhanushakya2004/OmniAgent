# Eggless Baking Substitution Rules & Ratios

> **Summary:** Comprehensive scientific conversion ratios for replacing whole eggs in cake sponges, cookies, and brownies.

## Instructions / Content

When baking eggless, eggs provide three primary functions: Moisture, Leavening (lift), and Emulsification (binding).
Use these exact substitutions per 1 large egg:
1. Dahi/Curd or Greek Yogurt: 1/4 cup (60g) thick curd + 1/4 tsp baking soda. Excellent for soft fluffy vanilla and fruit sponges.
2. Sweetened Condensed Milk: 1/4 cup (60g) condensed milk (reduce recipe sugar by 2 tablespoons). Provides rich caramel crumb and tender moisture.
3. Flax Egg (Vegan): 1 tbsp (10g) ground golden flaxseed powder + 3 tbsp warm water. Whisk and rest for 10 minutes until gelatinous. Superb for chocolate cakes, brownies, and nutty breads.
4. Vinegar & Baking Soda Reaction: 1 tbsp white vinegar or apple cider vinegar + 1 tsp baking soda. Chemical reaction releases carbon dioxide, producing tall airy sponge cakes without eggs.
5. Mashed Banana: 1/4 cup ripe banana. Best for pancakes and tea loaves (adds slight banana flavor).
