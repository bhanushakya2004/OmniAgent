# Authentic Mumbai Street Butter Pav Bhaji

> **Summary:** Classic Mumbai street food spiced vegetable mash served with toasted butter pav and lemon wedges.

## Ingredients

- **Everest Pav Bhaji Masala**: 30 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Amul Pasteurised Salted Butter Block**: 150 g (Butter & Margarine)
- **Dabur Hommade Ginger Garlic Paste**: 40 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Everest Kashmiri Lal Stemless Mild Red Chilli Powder**: 15 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Tata Salt Vacuum Evaporated Iodised Table Salt**: 10 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Fresh Farm Yellow Seedless Juicy Lemons**: 3 piece (Fresh Fruits)
- **Fresh Hydroponic Cleaned Coriander Leaves**: 50 g (Fresh Fruits)

## Instructions / Content

Spicy, buttery Mumbai street food classic.
1. Boil potatoes, cauliflower, and green peas until tender. Mash thoroughly.
2. Heat butter on a flat tava. Saute chopped onions, ginger garlic paste, and tomatoes until soft.
3. Add Everest Pav Bhaji masala and Kashmiri red chilli powder. Mash together with butter.
4. Add mashed vegetables with warm water and simmer for 15 minutes, mashing continuously.
5. Serve with generous dollop of Amul butter, chopped onions, and fresh lemon juice.
