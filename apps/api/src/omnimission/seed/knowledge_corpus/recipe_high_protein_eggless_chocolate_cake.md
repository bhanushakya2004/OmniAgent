# High-Protein Eggless Dark Chocolate Fudge Cake

> **Summary:** Rich decadent chocolate cake fortified with whey or plant protein powder, dark cocoa, and Greek yogurt moisture.

## Ingredients

- **Urban Platter Californian Superfine Blanched Almond Flour**: 150 g (Flours)
- **Cadbury Rich Cocoa Powder**: 50 g (Baking Essentials)
- **MuscleBlaze Raw Whey Protein Concentrate 80%**: 60 g (Protein Supplements)
- **Double Action Baking Powder**: 8 g (Baking Essentials)
- **Epigamia Natural Greek Yogurt High Protein Plain Curd**: 200 g (Yogurt & Curd)
- **Nestle Milkmaid Sweetened Condensed Milk**: 150 g (Baking Essentials)
- **Morde Real 55% Dark Chocolate Drops for Baking**: 100 g (Chocolate & Candies)
- **Sprig Natural Bourbon Vanilla Extract**: 5 ml (Essence & Flavors)

## Instructions / Content

A fitness-friendly protein dessert delivering 16g protein per slice.
1. Sift almond flour, cocoa powder, whey protein powder, and baking powder together.
2. In a separate bowl, blend Greek yogurt, condensed milk, and vanilla extract.
3. Fold wet and dry ingredients gently together with melted dark chocolate chips.
4. Bake in preheated oven at 175°C for 25 minutes. Serve warm with greek yogurt or fresh berries.
