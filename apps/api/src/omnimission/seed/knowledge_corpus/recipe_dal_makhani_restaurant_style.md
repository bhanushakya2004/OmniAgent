# Slow-Cooked Punjabi Dal Makhani

> **Summary:** Velvety black lentils and kidney beans slow-simmered overnight with butter, tomato puree, and fresh cream.

## Ingredients

- **Tata Sampann Skinned Whole White Urad Dal**: 200 g (Pulses & Dals)
- **Tata Sampann Kashmiri Speckled Chitra Rajma**: 50 g (Pulses & Dals)
- **Amul Pasteurised Salted Butter Block**: 80 g (Butter & Margarine)
- **Amul Pure Low Fat Fresh Dairy Cream**: 60 ml (Cream & Whipping)
- **MDH Aromatic Dried Fenugreek Leaves (Kasuri Methi)**: 10 g (Spices & Salt)

## Instructions / Content

1. Soak whole black urad and rajma overnight. Pressure cook until very soft.
2. Cook tomato ginger puree in butter with Kashmiri chilli powder.
3. Simmer dal on very low flame for 60 minutes, adding butter cubes until creamy and glossy.
