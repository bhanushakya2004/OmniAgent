# Traditional Amritsari Pindi Chole

> **Summary:** Dark robust chickpea curry simmered with black tea bag for rich dark color and pomegranate seed tanginess.

## Ingredients

- **Fortune White Chickpeas Big Kabuli Chana**: 300 g (Pulses & Dals)
- **MDH Chana Masala Spice Blend**: 30 g (Spices & Salt)
- **Brooke Bond Red Label Natural Care Masala Chai**: 10 g (Tea & Coffee)
- **Fortune Kachi Ghani Pure Mustard Oil**: 50 ml (Edible Oils)

## Instructions / Content

1. Soak kabuli chana overnight. Pressure cook with a black tea bag for authentic dark color.
2. In mustard oil, fry ginger juliennes, green chillies, and MDH chana masala.
3. Simmer cooked chickpeas with ground anardana and amchur for 20 minutes until thick.
