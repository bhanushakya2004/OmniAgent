# Grain-Free Keto Almond Butter Shortbread

> **Summary:** Melt-in-mouth 4-ingredient diabetic-friendly keto cookies with 3g net carbs per cookie.

## Ingredients

- **Urban Platter Californian Superfine Blanched Almond Flour**: 200 g (Flours)
- **Pintola Smooth Creamy All Natural Peanut Butter**: 80 g (Superfoods & Seeds)
- **Amul Pasteurised Salted Butter Block**: 60 g (Butter & Margarine)
- **Zydus Sugar Free Green Natural Stevia Powder**: 20 g (Sugars & Sweeteners)

## Instructions / Content

1. Beat softened butter and stevia until light.
2. Mix in almond butter and almond flour to form a pliable cookie dough.
3. Roll into 10 rounds, press with fork, and bake at 160°C for 14 minutes until edges turn golden.
